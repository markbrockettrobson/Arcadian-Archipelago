# Relics

## The Lore

Beneath the Arcadian Archipelago's jungle canopies, coral reefs, and volcanic stone lie the ruins of ancient civilisations. Some of the objects left behind still carry a living spark of old magic. Since the return of [The Red Wake](../Factions/The%20Red%20Wake.md), these relics have become highly sought after they can be bonded to a bearer and grow in power alongside them.

---

## Relic Seeds

A **relic seed** is a small, faintly magical object, a shard of engraved stone, a strange coin, a fragment of carved bone — that holds dormant potential. On its own it does very little, but when attached to an item it becomes a **relic**: a unique living magical item that grows in power through the bearer's choices.

### Attaching a Relic Seed

When you find a relic seed, you may attach it to **any item**. The seed and item then become inseparable;. That object is now your **relic**.

* Attaching a relic seed requires a short ritual (10 minutes)
* Once bonded, the seed cannot be moved to another item.
* If the item is destroyed, the seed survives and may be reattached.

### Relic Aspects

Every relic has **two aspects** themes that define what kind of power it can develop.

|||
|-|-|
|**Aspect 1**|Chosen by the GM. Revealed when the seed is attached.|
|**Aspect 2**|Chosen by the player, when you gain minor relic charm|

Aspects represent the soul of the relic (fire, shadow, plant, water, etc.) and determine which gifts it can develop. See [Relic Aspects](https://2e.aonprd.com/Relics.aspx) for the full list.

---

## Relic Gifts

As a relic grows it develops **gifts** active or passive abilities drawn from its aspects. Every relic begins with **one gift**.

## Relics are always your level. They use your spellcasting DC if you have one, otherwise your class DC. For spell attacks, use your DC −10 or your normal spell attack bonus.

## Relic Charms

Additional gifts are unlocked by finding and applying **relic charms**, rare objects hidden throughout the Archipelago.

When you apply a relic charm to your relic:

* Your relic gains **one new gift** of the charm's level.
* Gifts **alternate between your two aspects** in the order they are added.
* New gifts are selected by the player, guided by the GM.

### Charm Levels

|Charm Type|Gift Level|
|----------|----------|
|Relic Seed|Aspect 1 Minor Gift|
|Minor Relic Charm|Aspect 2 Minor Gift|
|Major Relic Charm|Aspect 1 Major Gift|
|Greater Relic Charm|Aspect 2 Major Gift|
|Grand Relic Charm|Aspect 1 Grand Gift|
|True Relic Charm|Aspect 2 Grand Gift|

Don't bet on getting True Relic Charms.

### Restrictions

* Each gift at a given level must be from a **different aspect**; you may choose a weaker gift in place of a stronger one.
* No two players may share an aspect. Wait until all seeds are distributed before choosing aspects for minor charms.

### Example Progression

 > 
 > A relic with aspects **Fire** and **Shadow** might grow like this:
 > 
 > |Step|Gift Level|Aspect|
 > |----|----------|------|
 > |Relic Seed|Minor|Fire|
 > |Minor Charm|Minor|Shadow|
 > |Major Charm|Major|Fire|
 > |Greater Charm|Major|Shadow|
 > |Grand Charm|Grand|Fire|
 > |True Charm|Grand|Shadow|

---

## Finding Relics

Relics are prizes for bonus objectives exploration, attention, and puzzle-solving. You will have more opportunities than needed to fully upgrade, so missing a few is fine. Charms are distributed in batches to keep things fair: all seeds first, then all minor charms, then major, and so on.
