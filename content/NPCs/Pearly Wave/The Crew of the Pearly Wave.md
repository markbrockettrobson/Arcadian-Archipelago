# The Crew of the Pearly Wave

The Pearly Wave is a worker-owned Horizon Line Cooperative vessel contracted to carry passengers and supplies in the expedition flotilla. Everyone aboard holds shares. Captain Maelis Thorn is crew-elected and serves at their confidence. Contract profits are split among the crew on return.

This is not how most ships work, and the crew know it. They are proud of it.

The atmosphere takes some getting used to. Crew members speak their minds, challenge decisions they disagree with, and hold a shipboard assembly every few days. It is louder than a conventional ship, but by most measures well run.

They have been living alongside you for weeks at sea. By now, most of them are familiar faces.
